PRECIPITATION PROBABILITIES

Event probability: P(observed precipitation event | forecast event). Hour probability: P(observed wet hour | forecast wet hour). Each intensity probability is threshold-calibrated: P(observed ≥/> threshold | forecast ≥/> the SAME threshold). Each threshold has its own forecast sample N -- thresholds do NOT share a denominator and their probabilities are not required to be monotonic (a stronger forecast signal can be better- or worse-calibrated than a weaker one). Maturation delay: an hour/event is only counted once real time has passed 12h past its own valid time/event end (late ARSO corrections do not retroactively change the ledger; --rebuild remains the safety net for rare late corrections). Lead range: 1–48 h. This is empirical history, not a ranking.

Categories: NEGLIGIBLE >0.1–0.3 mm/h; LIGHT >0.3–1.0 mm/h; MODERATE >1.0–<4.0 mm/h; HEAVY 4.0–10.0 mm/h; DOWNPOUR >10.0 mm/h.

WET forecast >0.1 → observed >0.1; LIGHT+ forecast >0.3 → observed >0.3; MODERATE+ forecast >1.0 → observed >1.0; HEAVY+ forecast >=4.0 → observed >=4.0; DOWNPOUR forecast >10.0 → observed >10.0 (mm/h).

MIXED PAIRS: P(observed reaches higher model's threshold | model A reaches threshold and model B forecasts exactly one category lower). Direction matters; model identities are not pooled -- CH1 higher/ECMWF lower is a different statistic from ECMWF higher/CH1 lower, and from CH1 higher/DWD lower. No WET Event row: DRY has no WET ModelEvent to overlap with, so WET mixed pairs exist for Hour only.
